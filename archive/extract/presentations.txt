## H1: Overview of Presentations

In each video below, scholars present how they have used video-based analysis in their past and present research. Altogether, these videos illustrate a diverse set of historical, contemporary and interdisciplinary approaches to video-based analysis that support an equally diverse set of research questions from different philosophical orientations.
